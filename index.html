How to use this page

Simply Live's setlists, with a reference link for every song. Opens on tonight's gig; use ‹ › or tap the date to change it.

Full view, for practising

Each song shows its key, version and singer, with buttons for its references: Spotify, YouTube, Audio or Score.

ref: Ori −2 means the key we play is 2 semitones lower than the original recording.

Songs highlighted like this don't have a link in the sheet yet.

Compact view, for performing

Each set fits on one screen. Scroll down and it snaps to the next set. Links are hidden and your screen stays on. Your phone remembers which view you used last.

Tip: add this page to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen). It then opens full-screen, which leaves room for bigger text.

When the setlist changes

The page reads the setlist doc every time you open it, refresh it or come back to it. Fetching the latest version takes about 10 seconds; until then you see the last copy saved on your phone. "Updated" at the bottom of the full view shows when it last checked.

Missing or broken links

Everything comes live from the setlist doc and Simply Live's Repertoire Master Sheet. Links can't be changed on this page.

All missing or broken links have to be added or fixed in that Google Sheet, in the LINKS column of the "SIMPLY LIVE Repertoire" tab. The change shows up here the next time the page updates.
